    How the data are made

    • Readings come from the OpenAQ API (version 3), updated once a day by an automated workflow.
    • Providers and licences: loading…
    • Our own rules, not part of the AQI scale: a value is shown only if at least 18 of the 24 hourly readings exist; averages are truncated to one decimal place; data older than 24 hours are hidden.
    • All 15 locations are AirGradient sensors, chosen on 13 November 2026. Their accuracy has not been checked against reference instruments, and one sensor cannot represent a whole city.
