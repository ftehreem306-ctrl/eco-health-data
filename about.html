About the initiative

Founder

Tehreem Fatima & Shaheer Murtaza Shehri are the founders of Eco-Health Action Hub. Tehreem Fatima is a final-year BS Medical Laboratory Technology student at NUMS and a climate-health activist. Her interests are cancer genetics, epigenetics and translational medicine. She is not a physician or epidemiologist. Shaheer Murtaza Shehri is a second-year BS Artificial Intelligence student at NUST and a climate-health & social activist.

How we choose the evidence

  • Every claim comes from an evidence table approved by the founder, with preference for WHO and IARC reports, systematic reviews and large cohort studies.
  • Each finding is labelled known, suspected or unknown, and we say whether it comes from people, animals or laboratory cells.
  • An association means two things occur together. It does not prove that one causes the other.
  • The full text, limitations and all references are in the white paper, and every reference is listed on the references page.
  • Known consistent evidence from large human studies or official evaluations
  • Suspected some human evidence, small or inconsistent, or mainly animal or laboratory work
  • Unknown no good source found

What this project does not do

  • It does not collect biological samples or genetic data.
  • It does not measure health outcomes at the monitoring locations.
  • It does not offer diagnostic tests or medical advice.

Limitations

  • Most human studies estimate exposure from home address or region rather than measuring it for each person.
  • Little of the genetic and epigenetic research comes from Pakistan. The Pakistani studies are small, focus on workers or were done in the laboratory.
Corrections are welcome: open an issue on GitHub.
